# Admin guide for the hosts

Sign in at **sirihomestay.in/admin**.

## When an enquiry arrives

It appears under **Overview** and **Bookings** as **New enquiry**, and (once email is set up) Swetha and Ganesh get an email with the guest's dates and phone number.

1. Open the enquiry. Call or WhatsApp the guest from the links on the page.
2. Choose the next step:
   - **Confirm booking**: tick the villa(s) or leave them unticked to use the first free ones. Tick "Email the guest" to send a confirmation.
   - **Hold for 48 hours**: keeps villas aside while the guest decides. The hold ends by itself and the villas are freed.
   - **Decline enquiry** if you can't host them.
3. Add the quoted price and any private notes under **Notes and quote**.

The system will not let two bookings or blocks use the same villa on the same night. If you try, it tells you which villa and which night clashes.

## Bookings taken by phone, WhatsApp or walk-in

**Bookings > Add a booking.** Choose the stay, dates, guests and how it came in. Choose **Confirmed** to block the villa straight away.

If someone books on Airbnb or MakeMyTrip, add it here too with the platform's booking ID, so this calendar stays complete. Those platforms aren't connected to this system yet.

## The calendar

**Calendar** shows every villa, night by night. Green is confirmed, blue is on hold, dark is checked in, striped gold is blocked. Select a coloured night to open its booking.

**Block dates** closes a villa (or all villas) for maintenance or your own use. "Until" is the morning the villa is free again.

**Download a villa's calendar** gives you an .ics file you can open in your phone's calendar.

## On the day

Open the booking and use **Check in**, then **Check out**. Use **Mark no-show** if the guest doesn't arrive.

## Changing what the website says

- **Stays and villas:** name, description, beds, sleeps-up-to, features list, and the rate line (either "Quoted by the hosts for your dates" or "From ₹… per …"). You can also rename villas or take one out of use.
- **Property and contacts:** check-in and check-out times, address, PIN code, map coordinates, licence number, estate facts, and the enquiry contacts in the order they appear.

## Showing your calendar to guests

**Settings > Show my calendar to guests.** Turn this on only once every booking, from every source, is entered here. Guests will then see full dates on the villa pages and can't send enquiries for dates that are full. While it's off, the website tells guests that you confirm availability personally.

## Airbnb and MakeMyTrip

Under **Airbnb and MakeMyTrip** you can record each listing's ID and link now. They stay marked **Not connected** until the connection is built in Phase 2.

## Emails and activity

- **Emails** lists every message the system created and whether it was sent.
- **Activity** records every change, who made it and when.

## Account

**Settings > Change password**, or **Sign out on every other device** if a phone or laptop is lost.
